Delete your data

Char Log keeps what you log on your phone. Without an account we don't hold a copy; if you sign in with Google, your logs also back up to your account.

In the app: Settings, Your data, Delete everything. It wipes workouts, meals, water, weight, your stats, targets and settings from the phone, for good.

Or uninstall the app: that also removes its data from the phone.

If Android backup is on for your phone, your Google account's backup may still hold a copy until it's replaced; you can manage that in your phone's Settings, Google, Backup.

Your account (if you signed in with Google): open the Circle tab, tap the menu at the top right, then Delete my account. Your account, your backup of your logs, your name, your circles, invites, reactions, nudges and every daily yes/no tick are deleted from our server straight away. Your logs on the phone stay.

To delete only the backup: Settings, Your data, Delete everything while signed in (this clears the phone too).

Can't open the app? Email us from the Google address you signed in with and we'll delete it within 7 days. Email charlogdev@gmail.com.
