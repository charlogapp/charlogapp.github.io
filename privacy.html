Char Log privacy policy

What the app stores, what goes online and what you can do about it, in plain words. Last updated 22 October 2026 (where to delete your account).

What Char Log stores

Everything you log: workouts, sets and routines, with the notes, gym names, photos, videos and locations you add to a workout; meals, your own foods and scanned packets; water; weight and waist; your stats (weight, height, age, sex, goal) and daily targets; reminders and settings.

All of it is kept in the app's storage on your phone. You don't need an account to log. If you sign in with Google, a copy backs up to your account (see below).

What goes online

Without an account, nothing you log is sent to us. There are no ads, no advertising ID and no analytics or tracking, ours or anyone else's, with or without an account.

These things use the internet, only when you use them:

  • Barcode lookup: the camera reads the barcode on your phone, and the picture never leaves it. Only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free, open food database, to look the packet up. A packet you scanned before is found on the phone, without the internet. What you then log stays on your phone.
  • Exercise photos: opening an exercise downloads its photo from GitHub, where the free exercise library is hosted. Like any website, GitHub can see your IP address; nothing about you is sent.
  • Talk with AI: builds a message from your workout and stats that you can read first. It leaves your phone only if you tap to open ChatGPT, copy it or share it, and then that app's privacy policy applies.
  • Workout location: only if you tap Add location. Your phone's location service (Google's, on most Android phones) works out where you are; Char Log saves the spot with that workout on your phone and sends it nowhere.

Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself, with no server involved.

Your account and backup (only if you sign in)

Signing in with Google creates your Char Log account. Our server (Supabase, in Mumbai) keeps your Google email and name, and a compressed copy of everything you log (listed above), so your logs come back when you sign in on any phone.

The backup is private to your account: no other user can see it. It updates a few seconds after you log something and when you leave the app. Workout photos and videos are not in it yet; they stay on the phone.

When you sign out, you choose whether this phone keeps your logs or is cleared. Your backup stays in your account until you delete it.

Circles (only if you join one)

Circles use your account. For circles our server also keeps: your display name and username; for each day, yes or no for whether you logged anything, a workout, food and water (weight only if you turn it on), and your streak count; the circles you are in, invite codes and invites; and what you share in a circle: posts, comments, reactions and @mentions, plus the notifications about them.

Sharing a workout: after a workout you can share a workout card to the circles you tick. It shows the workout's name and number, how long it took, the total weight lifted, the number of sets, any personal records, your best sets and the muscles worked. It never includes your body weight, meals, the gym's name, the time you trained, your location, photos or notes.

Your circle sees your first name, your username, those yes/no ticks, your streak and what you share with it. Posts stay in the circles you shared them with; someone who joins a circle later also sees what was shared there before. Never what you ate or how much, weights you log, photos, videos, notes or locations, unless you share them.

Your profile sections: in Settings, Manage your public profile, you can choose to show your circle this week's muscle map (sets per muscle), the milestones you unlocked and how many workouts you did this month. Each is off until you turn it on. Only the sections you turn on are sent to our server, as numbers only, and only people who share a circle with you can see them. Turning one off deletes it from our server.

Finding you: someone who types your exact username can see your name and username and invite you to a circle; you accept or ignore every invite. There is no search or list of people. You can turn either off in Settings, Manage your public profile.

You can report a person, a circle, a post or a comment, and block anyone (the ⋯ next to it). A report keeps who sent it, who and what it is about, the reason and your note, so we can review it; the person is not told who reported them. Reports of nudity, of a photo or information about you shared without your OK, or of someone who seems too young hide the item for everyone straight away; other reports hide it once two people report it. We review hidden items and remove what breaks the circle rules. Blocking hides you from each other in every circle.

Daily ticks are deleted after 35 days and notifications after 90 days. Removed posts and comments are kept privately for up to 180 days, as Indian law requires, and then deleted.

To delete your account: Settings, Delete my account (at the bottom), or email us. Your account, your backup, your posts, comments and reactions, and everything stored with them are deleted; your logs on this phone stay unless you also use Delete everything.

Circle rules and complaints

Before you share anything, you agree to the circle rules:

  • Be kind. No bullying, harassment or hate.
  • No nudity or sexual content.
  • Only share photos of other people with their OK.
  • Be yourself. Don't pretend to be someone else.
  • Nothing that puts children at risk.

Keeping it safe

Your logs live in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read.

Everything the app sends over the internet goes over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Our hosting provider encrypts stored data, and only your own sign-in can reach your backup. Without an account we keep nothing on servers, and your logs stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app.

Phone backup

If backup is on for your phone, Android may include Char Log's data in your own Google account backup, so it can come back on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is under your control; we can't see it.

Workout photos and videos are left out of that online backup to keep it small. They stay on this phone, and moving to a new phone by cable or Wi-Fi brings them along. Photos are saved without their hidden location data; videos are kept as your camera recorded them.

Your choices

  • See and fix anything you logged, right in the app.
  • Take a copy: Settings, Your data, Export my data (a JSON file plus spreadsheet files). Restore it on a new phone from the same place. Workout photos and videos aren't in the export yet.
  • Bring your history from another app: Settings, Your data, Import workouts from another app. The file is read on your phone and never uploaded; the workouts it adds are treated like the rest of your logs (and back up to your account if you're signed in). Undo it for 7 days from the same place.
  • Delete it: Settings, Your data, Delete everything. If you're signed in, this deletes your account's backup too. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone, not your backup.

Unless you sign in, we hold nothing of yours; if you do, see above. You can contact us with any question or complaint.

Age

Char Log is made for ages 13 and up. Under 18, daily targets are set for a growing body, with no calorie cut. Age is used only to set targets, and we don't ask for proof of it.

Health

Targets, tips and estimates are not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or are struggling with food or your body, talk to a doctor or dietitian first. Tele-MANAS is free, 24x7, in your language: call 14416.

When this changes

If anything else ever needs to leave your phone (cloud backup, for example), this policy will be updated first and the app will ask you.
