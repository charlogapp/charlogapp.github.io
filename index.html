char log

Gym log. Indian food. No judgment.

Char log kya bolenge? Let them talk. Log your own progress: sets in one tap, dal and roti in katori and roti, water, weight, and a streak that forgives. No ads, no sales calls, and your logs stay on your phone.

Bring your people. Make a circle with your gym buddy, roommate or family, or a jodi with one friend. They see a tick for each day you log and your streak. Never your meals, weights or photos. Circles need a Google sign-in; everything else works without an account.
